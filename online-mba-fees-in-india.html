Read This First

Online MBA fees are not one fixed number. Three things change them.

Specialisation and intake

Dual specialisations, premium tracks and different intakes can have different fees at the same university.

Offers and payment mode

Student grants, early-bird scholarships and lump-sum discounts change the amount you pay, and they expire.

Outdated listings

Many websites copy old fees. We found different totals for the same university even in 2026 listings, and show the range instead of picking one.

Charges That Are Often Not in the Headline Fee

Check each of these when you compare two universities.

Exam fees

Included in the semester fee at some universities (LPU, GLA) and charged separately at others.

Registration and application fee

One-time amounts, usually between ₹500 and ₹1,500, and non-refundable.

Alumni, ID or LMS charges

GLA lists a one-time alumni fee in Semester 1. Ask whether any similar fee applies.

Taxes

At least one listing for Chandigarh University says taxes are applicable on top of the fee.

EMI terms

“No-cost” EMI is offered through lending partners. Ask about processing fees, tenure and what happens if you drop out.

Dual specialisation or premium tracks

These can cost noticeably more than the standard Online MBA. Compare like with like.

How to Verify the Fee Before You Pay

Six steps we recommend to every student.

  1. Check recognition first. Confirm the programme and session on deb.ugc.ac.in before comparing fees.
  2. Ask for the fee sheet in writing for your exact specialisation and intake.
  3. Ask for the total payable, including exam, registration, alumni and taxes.
  4. Understand every discount: who qualifies, when it ends and whether it is refundable.
  5. Read the refund policy and the EMI agreement before you sign.
  6. Pay only on the university’s official payment page, never to a personal account.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Online MBA fees in India.

Which Online MBA has the lowest fee in India?

Among the six universities in this table, GLA’s reported total (₹1,05,300) is the lowest, but fees change and cheapest does not mean best. Compare recognition, curriculum, exam format and support alongside fees.

Why do I see different fees for the same university?

Fees differ by specialisation and intake, and discounts expire. Many third-party sites also publish outdated figures. Only a written fee sheet from the university is reliable.

Are Online MBA fees paid all at once?

Usually no. Most universities allow semester-wise or yearly payment, and some give a discount for paying in full. EMI is available through lending partners.

Is an Online MBA cheaper than a regular MBA?

Often yes, because there are no hostel or relocation costs, but not always. See our guide on regular vs online education fees.

Does a higher fee mean a better programme?

No. Fee alone does not show quality. Check UGC-DEB entitlement, curriculum, faculty, assessment method and student support.

Can I get a scholarship or discount?

Many universities offer grants, scholarships or early-bird discounts for limited periods. Eligibility and deadlines vary, so ask the university for the current terms in writing.
