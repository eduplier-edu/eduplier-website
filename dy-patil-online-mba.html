Frequently Asked Questions

Key clarifications regarding the online MBA from DY Patil University.

Is the Online MBA degree from DY Patil University valid for government jobs & higher studies?

Yes. As per UGC regulations, online degrees earned from UGC-DEB entitled and NAAC A++ accredited universities hold equal validity and recognition as on-campus regular degrees for both central/state government employment and further doctoral studies (Ph.D.).

Are working professionals required to attend live classes in person?

No. 100% of the instruction is conducted digitally. Live sessions occur on weekends, and session recordings are uploaded to the learning portal for on-demand playback whenever your work schedule allows.

How are semester examinations conducted?

Examinations are completely online and conducted through a remote, web-proctored secure examination portal. You can take your exams from home using a laptop or desktop with a functional webcam and stable internet.

Can I pay the total program fees in monthly EMI instalments?

Yes. Flexible 0% interest loan and credit EMI options spanning 12, 18, or 24 months are supported through university-approved banking partners.
