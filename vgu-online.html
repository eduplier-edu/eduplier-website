About VGU Centre for Distance & Online Education (CDOE)

Established under Rajasthan State Legislature Act No. 11/2012 and sponsored by the renowned Bagaria Education Trust (BET), Vivekananda Global University (VGU), Jaipur has grown into one of North India’s premier higher education hubs. In October 2022, VGU established the Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) to democratize flexible higher education across India and overseas.

Holding the prestigious NAAC Grade A+ accreditation in its very first cycle, VGU CDOE delivers digitally native undergraduate and postgraduate degree programs designed for working professionals, defense personnel, competitive exam aspirants (UPSC/State PSC), and career switchers.

Statutory Equivalence (UGC Regulation 22): Degrees earned through VGU Online CDOE carry identical legal validity and status as traditional on-campus degrees. Under UGC Open and Distance Learning and Online Programmes Regulations, VGU graduates qualify for UPSC Civil Services, SSC CGL, State PSC exams, PSU recruitment, and higher studies worldwide (WES recognized).

Frequently Asked Questions about VGU Online CDOE

1. Is Vivekananda Global University online degree valid for Government Jobs & UPSC?
Yes. VGU is established by a State Legislature Act and recognized under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act 1956. Furthermore, its online programs are formally entitled by the UGC-DEB (Distance Education Bureau). By law, these degrees hold 100% equivalence to on-campus degrees for UPSC, SSC, Banking, State PSCs, and Defense exams.
2. How are examinations conducted for VGU online programs?
All semester term-end examinations are conducted 100% online in remote proctored mode. Students do not need to travel to a physical test center in Jaipur. You only require a functional laptop or desktop with a webcam, microphone, and stable internet connection.
3. Can I pay the course fee in monthly installments?
Yes. VGU offers zero-interest (0% EMI) payment solutions through partner banking and NBFC networks. You can also pay your fee semester-by-semester rather than paying the entire program fee upfront.
4. What are the eligibility criteria for VGU Online MBA and MCA?
For Online MBA: Any graduate with a minimum 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories) from a recognized university. For Online MCA: Bachelor's degree (BCA, B.Sc Computer Science, or graduation with Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level) with 50% marks.
