Why Choose Mangalayatan University Online?

Tiered, budget-friendly online programmes from a NAAC A+ accredited university in Aligarh.

NAAC A+ Accredited

Mangalayatan University (established 2006, Aligarh) is listed as NAAC A+ accredited, and its online programmes are described as UGC-DEB entitled. Verify your programme and session on deb.ugc.ac.in.

Tiered Fee Options

General tracks are reported from about ₹35,000 to ₹70,000 in total. Plus and Pro tracks cost more. Confirm the current fee sheet for your track.

Flexible Online Learning

Online classes and online examinations, with direct merit-based admission reported and no entrance exam for most programmes.

Career Guidance

Ask the university what career support applies to online learners. No placement or job is guaranteed.

Semester-wise payment schedules and EMI facilities through university-approved partners.
