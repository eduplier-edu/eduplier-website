Frequently Asked Questions on Online Degree Admission

Yes. Employers across India accept an online degree admission because UGC Regulation 22 grants online programs equal standing with conventional degrees.

Most executives spend 6 to 8 hours each weekend reviewing recordings and completing assignments to pass after an online degree admission.

Are examinations conducted from home after online degree admission?

Yes. Accredited institutions conduct your examinations via web-proctored online platforms with flexible weekend test slots following your online degree admission.
