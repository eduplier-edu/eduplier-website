Steps to Complete Your Online Degree Admission

The entire online degree admission process is digital and can be completed in four straightforward steps:

  1. Program Selection: Choose the course and university that best match your career goals and budget.
  2. Document Submission: Upload your academic certificates, proof of identity, and photographs online.
  3. Eligibility Verification: The university verifies your qualification marks for your online degree admission.
  4. Enrolment & LMS Access: Pay your semester fee to confirm your online degree admission and unlock your course materials.

Frequently Asked Questions on Online Degree Admission

Is an online degree admission valid for government jobs?

Yes. Under UGC Regulation 22, completing an online degree admission from an entitled university qualifies you for UPSC, SSC, banking, and state government exams.

Do I need to take an entrance exam for an online degree admission?

Most universities offer direct online degree admission based on your marks in previous qualifying exams, without requiring CAT, MAT, or other tests.
