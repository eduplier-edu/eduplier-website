What Is an Online Degree?

An online degree is an undergraduate or postgraduate qualification offered by a recognised higher education institution through digital learning platforms. Instead of attending a physical classroom, you access lectures, study material, assignments and academic resources through an online learning management system (LMS).

This lets you study alongside a job, an internship, a business or other personal commitments.

LevelPopular online programmes
UndergraduateOnline BBA, BCA, BA and B.Com
PostgraduateOnline MBA, MCA, M.Com and MA, plus M.Sc. wherever permitted

Programme availability depends on each university's UGC-DEB recognition or entitlement for the relevant academic session.

Eligibility Criteria for Online Degree Admission

Eligibility differs by university and programme, but the general pattern is:

  • Undergraduate (BBA, BCA, BA, B.Com): Class 12 pass from a recognised board, with minimum marks as set by the university.
  • Postgraduate (MBA, M.Com, MA): A bachelor's degree from a recognised university, generally with a minimum aggregate, often around 50%.
  • MCA: A relevant bachelor's degree, often with mathematics at the Class 12 or graduation level, as the university specifies.
  • Entrance exam: Many online programmes admit students on previous marks, but confirm this for your chosen course.

Always check the latest eligibility on the university's official admission page.

Online Degree Admission Process

  1. Shortlist and verify universities and your programme on the UGC-DEB portal.
  2. Compare curriculum, fees, exam process and academic support.
  3. Generate your ABC ID and DEB ID, which UGC-regulated online admissions require.
  4. Apply online on the university's official admission portal.
  5. Upload documents: previous marksheets, qualification certificate, photo ID, photograph and signature.
  6. Pay the fee only through the official payment gateway, after checking the refund policy.
  7. Get verified and receive your LMS login to start learning.

Are Online Degrees Valid and UGC-Approved?

Yes, when the university is entitled. Under the UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations, 2020, a degree from a UGC-DEB entitled university is treated as equivalent to the corresponding conventional degree, subject to applicable conditions.

However, recognition is programme- and session-specific, and not every course can be offered online. Confirm your exact university and programme on deb.ugc.ac.in before you pay any fee.

How to Choose the Right Online University

  • Check UGC-DEB entitlement for your programme and session.
  • Match the course to your career goals and background.
  • Compare total fees, not just headline tuition.
  • Review the curriculum for current industry relevance.
  • Understand the exam method, such as online proctoring.
  • Check support: faculty access, doubt-clearing and career guidance. Placement assistance does not guarantee a job.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Which university is best for an online degree in India?

There is no single best university. Choose based on your course, budget, career goals and the university's UGC-DEB entitlement for your programme and session.

2. Are online degrees valid in India?

Yes, when offered by a university entitled or recognised by UGC-DEB for that programme and session, subject to applicable regulations.

3. Which online degree courses can I pursue?

Popular options include BBA, BCA, BA and B.Com at undergraduate level, and MBA, MCA, M.Com and MA at postgraduate level, depending on each university's availability.

4. Can I do an online degree while working?

Yes. Recorded lectures, live sessions and digital study material make it practical for many working professionals.

5. Is an entrance exam required?

Many programmes admit students on previous marks, but requirements vary. Check the official admission page of your chosen university.

6. How do I check if a university is UGC-DEB approved?

Visit deb.ugc.ac.in, select the relevant academic session and check the university's entitlement for your exact programme.
