Frequently Asked Questions

Key information regarding Chandigarh University Online programs.

1. Is an online degree from Chandigarh University valid for government jobs?

Yes. Chandigarh University is UGC-DEB approved and accredited with NAAC A+. Degrees issued through its online learning mode carry the same statutory status as regular degrees for UPSC, SSC, banking, state PSC, and corporate hiring.

2. How are examinations conducted?

All term-end exams are conducted online using an AI-monitored proctoring system. Learners can take tests from home using a desktop or laptop equipped with a functional camera and microphone.

3. Are flexible installment or EMI options available?

Yes. Students can pay course fees semester-wise. Additionally, zero-interest EMI financing plans are offered through university tie-ups with verified financial partners.

4. Does the university assist with jobs and internships?

Yes. The university provides placement guidance through virtual interview drives, profile assessments, and job portal access connecting candidates with corporate recruiters.

5. How does Eduplier assist during the admission journey?

Eduplier delivers free, transparent academic counselling. Advisors help you verify eligibility, select specializations, review fees, and navigate application verification without extra consultation costs.
