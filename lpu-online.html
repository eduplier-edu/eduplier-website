Common Questions About LPU Online

Is the LPU Online degree valid?

LPU Online programs are offered as UGC-entitled degrees. Our counsellors can share the latest approval details with you.

Who can apply for LPU Online?

Fresh graduates, working professionals and career switchers. Eligibility depends on the program, and we will guide you.

Can I study while working?

Yes. Online learning lets you study at your own pace, around your job or other commitments.
