Who is Online BA for?

Online BA in online mode is a good fit for 12th pass students who want a flexible undergraduate degree across humanities and social science subjects. Since classes and study material are accessible anytime, it removes the need to pause your current job, business or personal commitments to pursue a degree.

Eduplier works with multiple UGC-DEB approved universities offering this program, so you can compare options and choose one that fits your goals and budget rather than being limited to a single university.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Passed 10+2 (or equivalent) from a recognized board in any stream
  • Minimum aggregate marks as prescribed by the chosen university
  • No entrance exam required by most universities for online admission

Common Questions

Online BA — Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often from students exploring Online BA.

Is an online BA degree valid for government jobs and higher studies?

Yes, when pursued from a UGC-DEB approved university, an online BA carries the same recognition as a regular BA for most government exams and postgraduate admissions.

Which subjects can I choose in an online BA?

Common options include English, Economics, Political Science, Psychology, Sociology and History, though exact subject combinations vary by university.

Can I pursue an online BA alongside a job or another course?

Yes, this flexibility is one of the main reasons students choose the online mode for a BA.

What is the duration of an online BA?

The standard duration is 3 years, divided into semesters, same as a regular on-campus BA.

Can I pursue MA after completing an online BA?

Yes, an online BA is a valid pathway into MA and other postgraduate arts programs.
