6. UGC-DEB Approval: Are Manipal Online and LPU Online Recognised?

This is one of the most important things to check before taking admission. The University Grants Commission (UGC) regulates higher education in India, while the Distance Education Bureau (DEB) maintains information about eligible and recognised online and distance programmes. Do not assume that every course automatically has approval for every academic session.

Manipal University Jaipur UGC-DEB Status

The UGC-DEB programme listing identifies Manipal University Jaipur under the name Manipal University for online programmes, including the Online MBA in its 2025-26 listing. The university also states that its online programmes are UGC-entitled. For a later session, check the latest programme-specific entry before admission.

LPU UGC-DEB Status

The UGC-DEB recognition information lists Lovely Professional University for Online MBA and Online MCA for the recognition period extending through the January 2027 session. For other LPU programmes, check each programme's recognition and applicable session rather than assuming that MBA and MCA approval covers every course.

How to Check UGC-DEB Approval

  1. Visit deb.ugc.ac.in.
  2. Open the recognised or entitled Higher Education Institution programme list.
  3. Select the applicable academic year and session.
  4. Search for the university name.
  5. Find your selected course.
  6. Confirm the learning mode and recognition period.
Important: Previous-year recognition does not automatically mean a programme is approved for the next admission session. Always verify the current entry before paying admission fees.

12. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Which is better, Manipal Online or LPU Online?

Both universities offer online undergraduate and postgraduate programmes. Manipal Online has a lower published standard MBA fee in this comparison, while LPU offers its own range of courses and specialisations. The better option depends on your budget, eligibility and preferences.

2. What is the total fee for an Online MBA at Manipal University Jaipur?

The published standard fee is ₹46,000 per semester for four semesters, making the total tuition fee ₹1,84,000. An application fee of ₹500 is additional.

3. What is the total fee for an Online MBA at LPU?

LPU's published standard MBA fee is ₹50,000 per semester for four semesters, making the total tuition fee ₹2,00,000. A registration fee of ₹1,000 is additional.

4. Is an Online MBA from Manipal University Jaipur valid?

An Online MBA offered under the applicable UGC-DEB entitlement is valid. Verify the university and programme for your admission session through the official UGC-DEB portal.

5. Is an Online MBA from LPU valid?

LPU's Online MBA appears in the UGC-DEB recognition information for the applicable recognition period through January 2027. Verify the latest status for your intended session before applying.

6. What is the duration of an Online MBA at Manipal and LPU?

Both universities offer a two-year Online MBA programme consisting of four semesters.

7. Can working professionals pursue an Online MBA from Manipal or LPU?

Yes. Both universities offer online learning options that allow working professionals to continue their education while managing their jobs.

8. Is work experience compulsory for an Online MBA?

Work experience is not compulsory for admission to Manipal University Jaipur's Online MBA. LPU's general MBA eligibility also accepts qualifying bachelor's degree holders without listing work experience as a compulsory requirement.

9. Which university offers more Online MBA specialisations?

Both offer several specialisations in areas such as Finance, Marketing, Human Resources and Business Analytics. Options may change, so check the latest programme brochure.

10. How can I check whether an online university is UGC-DEB approved?

Visit deb.ugc.ac.in and search for the university and selected programme under the applicable academic year and session.

11. Can I get admission to Manipal Online or LPU without leaving my job?

Yes. Online programmes are designed to offer flexible learning. However, you must complete assignments, assessments and examinations according to the university schedule.
