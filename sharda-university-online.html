Accreditations and Approvals

Essential government and regulatory entitlements backing Sharda University Online degrees.

  • UGC Recognized. Recognized under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, 1956, with degree granting authority.
  • NAAC A+ Accredited. Certified with an A+ grade by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council.
  • AICTE Approved. Management and computer application programs align with apex council guidelines.
  • UGC-DEB Entitlement. Approved by the Distance Education Bureau to offer degree programs in full online (OL) mode.
Verify before enrolling: Always check the entitled status on the official portal at deb.ugc.ac.in and review disclosures directly on sharda.ac.in.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Eduplier the official website of Sharda University?

No. Eduplier is an independent education guidance platform. Admissions, academic delivery, evaluations, and degrees are administered directly by Sharda University. Visit sharda.ac.in for official notifications.

2. Are Sharda University online degrees valid for jobs and government exams?

Yes. As per UGC regulations, online degrees earned from recognized universities entitled by the UGC Distance Education Bureau (UGC-DEB) hold full equivalence with regular on-campus degrees for private corporate employment, PSU recruitments, and government exams.

3. Which online programs does Sharda University offer?

Sharda University offers popular UGC-entitled programs including Online MBA, Online MCA, Online BBA, Online BCA, Online B.Com, and Online M.Com. Availability and specializations vary by academic session.

4. What is the fee structure for Sharda University online programs?

Fees depend on the chosen course, degree level, and semester schedule. Most undergraduate programs range from ₹90,000 to ₹1,50,000 in total, while postgraduate programs typically range between ₹1,00,000 and ₹1,80,000. Contact an Eduplier counsellor for an exact, updated fee sheet.

5. Can working professionals pursue Sharda University online degrees?

Yes. The programs feature asynchronous learning through an advanced Learning Management System (LMS), interactive live weekend masterclasses, and flexible digital study materials tailored for working schedules.
