Amity Online vs LPU Online MBA: Detailed Fee, Syllabus, and Placement Comparison

Choosing to pursue an MBA is one of the most practical investments a working professional or fresh graduate can make. When looking at online degree providers in India, two universities come up again and again: Amity University Online and Lovely Professional University (LPU) Online.

Both institutions are recognised by the relevant authorities and provide full digital learning platforms. There are clear differences between them, especially in fees, the specialisations offered, international recognition and help with jobs after graduation.

Quick take

LPU Online is the more budget-friendly option and holds a higher NAAC grade (A++). Amity Online costs more but brings stronger brand recall, WES entitlement for study or work abroad and a wider choice of specialised tracks. The right pick depends on your budget, career goals and plans.

In this comparison we look closely at cost, accreditation, course structure and placement support, so you can decide which university is better for your career goals and financial situation.

1. Quick Overview & Recognitions

Before looking at fees or courses, the first thing to check is whether the degree is valid. Both Amity University Online and LPU Online are entitled by the UGC Distance Education Bureau (UGC-DEB). This means their online MBA degrees carry the same legal standing as on-campus degrees across India, and are generally accepted for private-sector jobs and for government jobs and exams, subject to the eligibility rules of the employer or exam body.

2. Fee Structure & Financing Options

Cost is often the main deciding factor between the two. LPU Online positions itself as an affordable, high-value option, whereas Amity Online commands a premium fee that reflects its established brand.

LPU Online MBA

  • Total fees generally range from ₹1,20,000 to ₹1,60,000 for the 2-year program.
  • Payable semester-wise at roughly ₹30,000–₹40,000 per term.

Amity Online MBA

  • Total fees typically range from ₹1,99,000 to ₹2,80,000.
  • The amount depends on whether you choose a core track or a specialised industry track with international certifications.

Fees are indicative and may change. Please confirm the current fee structure with the university or your Eduplier counsellor.

Payment flexibility and no-cost EMIs

Both universities understand that paying all the fees at once is difficult for many professionals:

  • No-cost EMI: Both work with education NBFCs (such as LiquiLoans, Propelld and GrayQuest) to offer zero-interest monthly payment plans that start at around ₹5,000 to ₹8,000 per month, subject to approval.
  • Scholarships: LPU gives discounts for defence personnel, persons with disabilities and students with strong academic records. Amity offers scholarships for people who work with partner companies, alumni and students with strong undergraduate scores.

3. Syllabus and Specialisations

Both universities run the 2-year Online MBA across 4 semesters. Semesters 1 and 2 cover core business topics (Managerial Economics, Corporate Finance, Organisational Behaviour, Business Analytics and Marketing Management), while Semesters 3 and 4 focus on electives, specialisation subjects and a final project.

Amity Online MBA specialisations

  • Marketing and Sales Management
  • Finance and Accounting
  • Human Resource Management
  • Data Science and Business Analytics
  • Digital Marketing
  • International Business and Global Finance
  • Retail and Supply Chain Management

What stands out: live sessions with international faculty and industry leaders, plus certifications aligned with industry needs. Some tracks allow dual specialisation.

LPU Online MBA specialisations

  • Human Resource Management
  • Marketing Management
  • Financial Markets and Banking
  • Data Science and Operations
  • Digital Marketing
  • Information Technology
  • International Business

What stands out: subjects built around in-demand roles in the Indian industry, with recorded classes and regular tests on LPU e-Connect that suit people with irregular work hours or shifts.

4. Learning Experience: LMS & Examinations

Because you never need to visit a campus, the quality of the Learning Management System (LMS) shapes your day-to-day experience.

  • Live and recorded balance: Both platforms offer weekend live classes and keep recordings for later. If your job involves travel or weekend shifts, both LMS portals let you complete coursework and assignments online at any time.
  • Examination format: You do not need to go to a physical exam centre. Both universities run end-term exams on proctored web software. You need a desktop or laptop with a working camera, microphone and a steady internet connection.

5. Placements & Career Support: What to Expect

Students often ask: “Do online universities really offer placements?” Neither university guarantees 100% placement, and you should be careful of any consultant who says otherwise. However, both provide organised virtual career centres.

Amity Online career services

  • Virtual placement drives: access to Amity’s recruiter network during virtual hiring events.
  • Career readiness training: resume improvement, portfolio building and LinkedIn profile support.
  • Networking: access to an international alumni network in multinational companies.

LPU Online career services

  • Skill assessment and grooming: modules on business communication, aptitude test prep and mock interviews.
  • Placement assistance: recruitment drives where selected corporate partners hire for entry-to-mid-level remote or on-site business roles.
  • Mentorship: one-on-one guidance from industry mentors to help mid-career professionals move into leadership roles.

Verdict: Which Online University Should You Pick?

Pick the Amity Online MBA if:

  1. You want a well-known brand and credentials that count for evaluations such as WES.
  2. You like having specialised tracks, for example Global Finance or AI/Data Science, and you can afford a higher fee.
  3. You want networking opportunities linked to one of India’s large private university alumni groups.

Choose the LPU Online MBA if:

  1. You are looking for a cost-effective, high-ROI degree from a university with NAAC A++ accreditation.
  2. You want a flexible, well-organised LMS to earn a recognised degree without going over a budget of about ₹1.5 lakh.
  3. You want practical, career-focused guidance tailored to the Indian corporate and IT ecosystem.

Frequently Asked Questions

General & recognition

1. Are online degrees legally valid and recognised in India?

Yes. Under UGC regulations, online degrees awarded by UGC-DEB entitled universities carry the same legal validity as regular, full-time campus degrees. They are recognised for corporate employment, private-sector promotions and government jobs, subject to the employer’s own criteria.

2. Are online degrees accepted for government jobs, exams (like UPSC) and higher studies?

Yes. Because UGC treats approved online degrees on par with traditional degrees, graduates can appear for competitive exams like UPSC CSE, SSC, state PSCs and banking exams, and apply for postgraduate or doctoral studies, provided the eligibility criteria are met.

3. Will the degree certificate state “Online”?

Yes. Most UGC-compliant universities mention the mode of education (Online Mode) on the degree certificate and marksheet, along with the program name and enrolment details.

4. Are these degrees accepted abroad for immigration or higher education?

Institutions with global recognition or evaluation approvals (such as WES verification for institutions like Amity Online) are accepted for immigration evaluations (for example, Express Entry in Canada) and international admissions. Recognition depends on the evaluating agency in the destination country.

Academics, LMS & examinations

5. How are classes conducted?

Learning happens entirely through the university’s Learning Management System (LMS) or mobile app. Universities provide a blend of scheduled live interactive classes on weekends or evenings and recorded video lectures, e-books and study modules available 24/7.

6. Do I need to visit the university campus for examinations?

No. End-term examinations and continuous assessments are conducted online on AI-proctored platforms. You can take your exams from home using a laptop or desktop with a working webcam, microphone and a stable internet connection.

7. Can working professionals balance an online degree with their job?

Yes. Most programs are designed for working individuals and need about 6–10 hours of study per week. The recorded lectures let you learn at your own pace without disrupting your work schedule.

Fees, EMI & financials

8. What is the average fee for an online degree?

Undergraduate (BBA, BCA, B.Com, BA): roughly ₹90,000 to ₹1,80,000 in total across 3 years. Postgraduate (MBA, MCA, M.Com, MA): roughly ₹1,10,000 to ₹2,80,000 in total across 2 years, depending on the university and track chosen.

9. Are monthly EMI or payment options available?

Yes. Most universities offer semester-wise fee payment. In addition, partner NBFCs provide no-cost or low-cost monthly EMI options, so you can pay the program fee in manageable instalments of about ₹4,000 to ₹8,000.
