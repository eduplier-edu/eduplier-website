About Sikkim Manipal University

Established in 1995 in partnership with the Government of Sikkim, Sikkim Manipal University has educated over 5 lakh distance and online alumni. It offers highly affordable, career-oriented degrees in arts, commerce and management, with a semester fee structure that suits working individuals.

  • Flagship programs: Online BA, B.Com, MA (English / Pol. Science / Sociology), M.Com
  • Affordable semester fee structure ideal for working individuals
  • NAAC A+ accredited with an extensive national alumni network
  • Part of the Manipal Education Group, with 70+ years of educational heritage

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about admissions, degree validity and examinations.

Is an online degree from Sikkim Manipal University valid?

Yes. SMU holds UGC-DEB entitlement and NAAC A+ accreditation, and its online degrees are treated on par with regular degrees under UGC regulations. Please verify the latest status on the official UGC-DEB portal before applying.

Which online programs does SMU offer?

Popular programs include Online BA, B.Com, MA (English, Political Science or Sociology) and M.Com. Our counsellors can confirm which programs are currently open for admission.

Are the degrees valid for government jobs?

In line with UGC regulations, UGC-DEB entitled online degrees are treated on par with regular degrees for state and central government exams such as UPSC, SSC, Banking and Railways. Always check the specific recruiter's eligibility notice.

Can I take my examinations from home?

Yes. Term-end exams are conducted online through a remote web-proctored portal. You need a laptop or desktop with a webcam, microphone and stable internet.

Are EMI or semester-wise payment plans available?

Yes. Fees can be paid semester by semester, and zero-cost EMI is available through authorised educational loan partners.
