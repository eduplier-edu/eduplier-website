Is "Online Mode" Mentioned on the Degree Certificate?

Yes. As per the regulations laid down by the University Grants Commission (UGC), it is mandatory for recognized Higher Educational Institutions (HEIs) to mention "Online Mode" on your final degree certificate, transcript, and marksheets.

Many learners hold a misconception that their digital degree will be indistinguishable from on-campus, traditional degrees on the front. However, UGC's official mandates require full transparency regarding the delivery format. This labeling distinguishes 100% online learning from conventional classroom degrees and open-and-distance hybrid (ODL) models.

💡 Fast Fact: You do not need to worry about "Online Mode" labeling. It acts as official validation that your degree was delivered via a legally permitted virtual model approved by the UGC-DEB, rather than an unaccredited private training course.

The Mandatory UGC Regulations (Clauses Revealed)

The mandatory design parameters of online credentials are governed under the UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations, 2020. Key statutory details include:

📄 UGC Regulation 16(2)

This rule mandates that all HEIs must print the exact delivery mode—specifically labeled as "Online Mode"—the student’s registration/admission dates, completion date, and specific evaluation and examination centers directly on the certificate and transcripts.

🛡️ UGC Regulation 16(1)

Requires Universities to directly upload all degree certificates, provisional awards, and semester-wise marksheets to the National Academic Depository (NAD) / DigiLocker. This guarantees verify-by-employer functionality and protects against fake credentials.

  • Mention of NAAC ranking and UGC-DEB approval references

The Power of Legal Parity (UGC Regulation 22)

The most important aspect for students to know is that the label does not degrade your degree's value.

Under Regulation 22 of the UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations, 2020:

"Degrees at undergraduate and postgraduate levels... awarded through Open and Distance Learning mode and/or Online mode by Higher Educational Institutions, recognised by the Commission... shall be treated as equivalent to the corresponding awards of the Degrees at undergraduate and postgraduate levels... offered through conventional mode."

This guarantees that whether you hold a standard physical degree or an "Online Mode" degree, you are legally entitled to the exact same academic and employment honors. Let’s compare both formats:

Essential Checks to Guarantee Your Degree is Valid

The only factor that renders an online degree "invalid" is enrolling in an unaccredited institution. Always verify your potential university with this list:

  • 🔍 1. UGC-DEB Approval:
    Verify that your university is actively listed on the official DEB portal for online education (deb.ugc.ac.in) for your specific admission year.
  • 🎓 2. Specialisation Legality:
    Ensure your chosen field can legally be offered online. Technical/practical courses (such as engineering, law, nursing, medicine, or pharmacy) are strictly banned in online mode by the UGC.
  • 🏛️ 3. NAAC and NIRF Rankings:
    Universities must have a high rating (usually NAAC A+ or better, or a place in the NIRF top 100) to be allowed to run online programs without extra applications.
  • 📅 4. Specific Admission Term check:
    Approval is granted term-by-term. Do not assume previous semester's approval applies to your 2027 intake without reviewing current certificates.

Can you actually get jobs with this cert? Let’s check what recruiters and systems say:

👮 Government Jobs & Exams

UPSC Civil Services (IAS/IPS/IFS), SSC, Bank PO, and state PSC systems explicitly accept eligible graduates from UGC-DEB approved programs on exact par with campus degrees.

💼 Private & Global Enterprise

Corporate employers in India (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, etc.) and abroad focus entirely on skills, digital verify check on DigiLocker, and accredited status rather than delivery mode.

Keep in mind: In virtually all cases, if an HR portal or recruiter runs an verify check, it hits the DigiLocker and UGC registry to look for approval, which will succeed for your accredited degree.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Does an online degree certificate say "Online Mode" in India in 2027? ▼

Yes, as per UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations, it is mandatory for universities to mention "Online Mode" on the degree certificate, transcript, and marksheets—typically on the back or within the details of the credential.

2. Is a degree with "Online Mode" written on it valid for government jobs? ▼

Yes. Under Regulation 22 of the UGC 2020 Regulations, degrees earned through UGC-DEB recognized online or ODL modes are completely equivalent to standard regular, on-campus degrees and are fully accepted for all government exams (UPSC, SSC, Banks) and jobs.

3. What information must be printed on an online degree certificate according to UGC? ▼

The certificate must include the program name, the "Online Mode" delivery statement, the admission and completion dates, the examination/evaluation centres, and the university's UGC-DEB approval details.

4. Is "Online Mode" printed on the front or the back of the certificate? ▼

University patterns vary; some include it within the program description on the front (e.g., "Bachelor of Business Administration (Online Mode)"), while many put the mandatory delivery specifics, evaluation centers, and security features on the back.

5. Will an online degree specify "distance learning" or "external"? ▼

No, if you are enrolled in a 100% online program, the mode must be labeled explicitly as "Online Mode". "Open & Distance Learning (ODL)" is used exclusively for hybrid/correspondence distance programs.

6. Are online degrees uploaded to the National Academic Depository (NAD)? ▼

Yes, Regulation 16(1) of the UGC mandate requires universities to upload student degrees and transcripts to NAD / DigiLocker, making verify-by-employer seamless.
