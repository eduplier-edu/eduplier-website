Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you apply.

Is the online MBA UGC approved?

DPU’s online MBA is reported as UGC-entitled, and the university is NAAC A++ accredited. Please verify current status on the UGC-DEB portal.

Do I need CAT or MAT scores?

Public listings say entrance exams are not required for the online MBA. Confirm with the university.

Is this the same as D Y Patil University, Ambi?

No. Online programs are offered by Dr. D. Y. Patil Vidyapeeth, Pune (DPU). Other D Y Patil institutions are separate.

Is the degree valid for jobs and higher studies?

UGC-entitled online degrees are generally treated as equivalent. Individual employers or recruiters may set their own criteria.
