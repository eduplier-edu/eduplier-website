Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common student inquiries before applying.

Is Dr. D. Y. Patil Vidyapeeth online MBA UGC approved?

Yes. DPU’s online degree programs are entitled by the UGC Distance Education Bureau (DEB). The university is also accredited with NAAC A++ grade, ensuring legal parity with on-campus qualifications.

Do I need CAT, MAT, or CMAT entrance scores?

No. Standard entrance exam scores like CAT or MAT are not mandatory for admission to the DPU Online MBA. Candidates are evaluated on graduation aggregate marks and basic document verification.

Is this the same as D. Y. Patil University, Ambi?

No. Online degrees are offered specifically by Dr. D. Y. Patil Vidyapeeth, Pune (DPU Centre for Online Learning). Other D. Y. Patil institutions operate independently under separate statutory approvals.

Is the degree recognized for corporate jobs and PSU exams?

Yes. Under official UGC regulations, online degrees earned from entitled universities maintain full parity for private corporate recruitments, state government examinations, and UPSC.
