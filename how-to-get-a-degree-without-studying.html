How to Get a Degree Without Studying: What Actually Works (and What’s a Scam)

Few education searches are as popular as “how to get a degree without studying.” Working professionals, parents, and students who have lost interest in classroom learning all type it in, hoping for a shortcut.

Here is the honest answer: no legitimate university will give you a degree without any learning or assessment. Anything that promises one is a scam, and it can cost you your money, your job, and your reputation.

But if what you really want is a degree without the traditional grind of daily classes, rigid schedules, and relearning things you already know, there are real, recognized ways to do it. This guide covers them.

Quick take

You can’t skip learning, but you can skip wasted effort. Open and distance learning, UGC-approved online degrees, the Academic Bank of Credits, SWAYAM credits and recognition of prior learning all let you study less of what you already know, on your own schedule.

First, Why “No Studying” Is a Trap

Websites advertising “instant degrees” or a “degree in 7 days” are usually diploma mills. Employers, universities, and visa authorities can verify credentials, and a fake one can lead to:

  • Job termination or blacklisting
  • Rejection of immigration or higher-study applications
  • Legal action for submitting false documents
  • Money lost with no refund

The smart goal isn’t to avoid learning. It’s to avoid wasting time on what you already know and to study in a way that fits your life.

7 Legitimate Ways to Earn a Degree Faster and More Flexibly

1

Open and Distance Learning (ODL)

Universities like IGNOU and state open universities offer recognized degrees with no daily attendance. You study from provided material, attend optional counselling sessions, and appear for exams.

Best for: Working professionals who need maximum flexibility.
2

UGC-Approved Online Degrees

Online degrees from UGC-DEB approved universities are recognized as equivalent to on-campus degrees. Lectures are recorded, so you learn when it suits you.

Best for: People who want a regular degree without relocating or quitting a job.
3

Credit Transfer through the Academic Bank of Credits (ABC)

Under NEP 2020, credits you earn can be stored digitally and transferred between institutions. You can pause your studies and resume later without losing progress.

Best for: Students who dropped out or need to take a break.
4

SWAYAM and Online Course Credits

SWAYAM offers free courses from top Indian institutions. Many universities accept credits from these courses toward a degree, subject to their own rules, so you can finish parts of a degree outside the classroom.

Best for: Self-motivated learners who want to cut down on classroom hours.
5

Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)

If you have years of hands-on experience, skill-certification schemes can formally recognize it. This is especially useful in vocational and technical fields and can act as a stepping stone to a diploma or degree.

Best for: Skilled workers without formal qualifications.
6

Credit for Experience and Competency-Based Programs (Abroad)

Some universities, mainly in the US and elsewhere, award credit for work experience, military training, or professional certifications. Others let you test out of courses through exams like CLEP or DSST, and progress by proving competency rather than sitting through fixed course durations.

Best for: Experienced professionals considering international options.
7

Fast-Track and Accelerated Programs

Some universities offer year-round terms, bridge programs, or accelerated formats that let motivated students finish in less time than usual.

Best for: Students who can commit intensive effort for a shorter period.

How to Spot a Fake Degree Offer

Walk away if you see any of these red flags

  • A degree in days or weeks with no coursework
  • A flat fee per degree instead of per semester or course
  • No recognition from UGC, AICTE, or the relevant national regulator
  • Credit for “life experience” with no assessment
  • Pressure tactics like “Offer ends tonight!”
  • No physical address, faculty details, or verifiable contact information

A 4-Step Checklist Before You Enroll

  1. Check recognition. Verify the university and the specific program on the official UGC and UGC-DEB websites.
  2. Confirm the degree’s validity for your goal: jobs, government exams, or higher studies abroad.
  3. Read the assessment details. Real programs always have exams, assignments, or projects.
  4. Ask about credit transfer and exemptions to see how much of your prior learning counts.

The Smarter Question to Ask

Instead of asking “How can I get a degree without studying?” try:

“How can I get an accredited degree while studying less of what I already know, and on a schedule that works for me?”

That question leads to real answers: flexible programs, credit transfer, experience-based recognition, and self-paced learning.

Final Thoughts

There is no legitimate way to earn a degree with zero effort, but there are many ways to earn one with less friction, more flexibility, and far less wasted time. Choose a recognized program, use the credit and recognition systems available to you, and make sure your degree can stand up to verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I really get a degree without studying?

No. Genuine degrees require learning and assessment. What you can do is reduce unnecessary study through credit transfer, recognition of prior learning, and flexible or self-paced formats.

2. Are online degrees valid in India?

Yes, provided they come from universities approved by UGC-DEB. Such degrees are treated as equivalent to conventional degrees. Always verify approval before enrolling.

3. Is a distance degree from IGNOU accepted for jobs?

IGNOU degrees are recognized by UGC and widely accepted for jobs and higher education. Some specific employers or exams may have their own rules, so check the requirements for your target role.

4. What is the Academic Bank of Credits (ABC)?

It is a digital system under NEP 2020 that stores the academic credits you earn so they can be transferred across participating institutions, letting you pause and resume your education.

5. How can I get a degree while working full-time?

Choose online or distance programs with recorded lectures and flexible exam schedules, and plan a fixed weekly study routine around your job.

6. How do I know if a university is fake?

Check for UGC or AICTE recognition, a verifiable address, clear fee structures, transparent exams, and no promises of instant degrees. A program offering a degree without any assessment is almost certainly fake.

7. What happens if I use a fake degree?

Consequences can include termination, legal action, blacklisting, and visa or admission rejection, and these can follow you for years.

8. Can I finish a degree faster than usual?

Sometimes. Accelerated programs, credit transfer, exemptions, and year-round terms can shorten the timeline, but regulations differ by institution.
