About Manipal University Jaipur

Established in 2011, Manipal University Jaipur has become one of India's leading institutions for corporate, technology and business education. Its online MBA and MCA tracks are built around current industry specializations, and learners get free access to Coursera certifications.

  • Flagship programs: Online MBA, MCA, BBA, BCA and M.Com
  • Free access to Coursera certifications
  • NAAC A+ Grade (CGPA 3.28) and NIRF ranked
  • Part of the Manipal Education Group, with 70+ years of educational heritage

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about admissions, degree validity and examinations.

Is an online degree from Manipal University Jaipur valid?

Yes. MUJ holds UGC-DEB entitlement and NAAC A+ accreditation, and its online degrees are treated on par with regular degrees under UGC regulations. Please verify the latest status on the official UGC-DEB portal before applying.

Which online programs does MUJ offer?

Popular programs include Online MBA (14+ specializations), MCA, BBA and BCA. M.Com is also offered. Our counsellors can confirm what is currently open for admission.

Are the degrees valid for government jobs?

In line with UGC regulations, UGC-DEB entitled online degrees are treated on par with regular degrees for state and central government exams. Always check the specific recruiter's eligibility notice.

Can I take my examinations from home?

Yes. Term-end exams are conducted online through an AI-proctored remote portal. You need a laptop or desktop with a webcam, microphone and stable internet.

Are EMI or semester-wise payment plans available?

Yes. Fees can be paid semester by semester, and zero-cost EMI is available through authorised educational loan partners.
