Eduplier (“we”, “our”, “us”) is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you interact with our website https://www.eduplier.com/ (the “Site”) and related services. By using the Site, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1

Information We Collect

We collect information to provide you with a better learning and service experience. This includes:

a. Personal Information You Provide

  • Name, email address, phone number, and other contact details
  • Information you submit through contact forms, newsletter sign-ups, or inquiries
  • Any other details you choose to provide while interacting with our services

b. Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our Site, we may automatically collect certain information, such as:

  • IP address, device type, and browser details
  • Pages you visit and how long you spend on the Site
  • Interactions with our content and features

You may manage or revoke these permissions through your device settings at any time. Disabling some permissions may limit functionality.

c. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to:

  • Analyze user behavior on our Site
  • Improve our services and personalize your experience
  • Support marketing, advertising, and analytics tools, including Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Google Search Console (GSC), and Ads platforms

You can manage cookies through your browser settings, but some Site features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

2

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

Provide, operate, and improve our educational services and website
Respond to inquiries, support requests, and user feedback
Communicate updates, newsletters, and opted-in promotional content
Analyze trends, usage, and performance to enhance user experience
Ensure the overall security and integrity of our online services
We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Sharing Information

We may share information with:

All third-party partners are required to maintain confidentiality and are strictly prohibited from using your personal data for any other purposes.

4

5

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, or as required by applicable laws and regulations.

6

Our services are intended for users aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we discover any such data has been collected inadvertently, we will promptly delete it.
