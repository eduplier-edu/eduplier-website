Who is Online M.Com for?

Online M.Com in online mode is a good fit for commerce graduates who want to deepen their expertise in accounting, finance and taxation for career or academic advancement. Since classes and study material are accessible anytime, it removes the need to pause your current job, business or personal commitments to pursue a degree.

Eduplier works with multiple UGC-DEB approved universities offering this program, so you can compare options and choose one that fits your goals and budget rather than being limited to a single university.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Bachelor's degree in Commerce, or a related discipline as prescribed by the university
  • Minimum aggregate marks as prescribed by the chosen university
  • Some universities may require a commerce background for admission

Common Questions

Online M.Com — Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often from students exploring Online M.Com.

Is an online M.Com valid for competitive exams and higher studies?

Yes, when pursued from a UGC-DEB approved university, an online M.Com carries the same recognition as a regular M.Com.

Do I need a B.Com background for online M.Com?

Most universities prefer a commerce background, but some accept related disciplines. It's best to confirm eligibility with the specific university.

What is the duration of an online M.Com?

The standard duration is 2 years, divided into semesters, same as a regular on-campus M.Com.

Can I pursue online M.Com while working in finance or accounting roles?

Yes, this is a common path for working professionals who want to formalize and deepen their commerce education.

Which specializations are available in online M.Com?

Common options include Accounting & Finance, Taxation, Banking & Insurance and Business Economics, varying by university.
