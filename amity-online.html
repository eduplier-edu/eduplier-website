Frequently Asked Questions

Are degrees from Amity University Online valid for government jobs & higher studies?

Yes. Amity University Online is entitled by the UGC-Distance Education Bureau (DEB). According to UGC regulations, online degrees from recognized universities hold equivalent status to conventional degrees for government exams, employment, and higher education abroad.

How are examinations conducted?

Examinations are held online via proctored assessment software. Students can schedule and complete their term-end examinations securely from home without visiting a physical exam centre.

Can I pay the program fee in monthly EMI installments?

Yes, no-cost EMI and installment options are available through partner financial institutions, allowing students to pay their fees comfortably on a month-to-month basis.
