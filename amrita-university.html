Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you apply.

Are Amrita’s online degrees UGC approved?

Amrita Online programs are reported as UGC-entitled, and the university holds NAAC A++ accreditation. Please verify current status on the UGC-DEB portal.

How are exams conducted?

Public listings say Amrita conducts online proctored exams for each course. Confirm the exam schedule with the university.

Is Amrita Online different from the campus programs?

Amrita Online is the online division of Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham. It runs separately from full-time campus programs, so check eligibility and fees for the online program you want.

Is the degree valid for jobs and higher studies?

UGC-entitled online degrees are generally treated as equivalent. Individual employers may set their own criteria, and the MBA is reported as WES-recognised for study abroad.
