Frequently Asked Questions

Key details regarding admissions, exams, and curriculum at NMIMS CDOE.

1. Is an online/distance degree from NMIMS CDOE valid for corporate careers?

Yes. NMIMS is a Category-I Autonomous University accredited with NAAC Grade A+ (3.59 CGPA) and entitled by UGC-DEB. Programs are widely accepted across corporate firms, multinational companies, and for further educational pathways.

2. How are examinations conducted?

Examinations are held twice a year. Students have the flexibility to appear for examinations online via an AI-proctored system or at designated examination centers depending on the session guidelines.

3. Can I pay course fees in installments?

Yes, semester-wise and annual payment modes are available. Additionally, zero-interest EMI financing options are provided through university financial tie-ups.

4. What career services does NMIMS CDOE offer?

NMIMS offers a dedicated career services package that includes job board access, corporate mentorship sessions, resume building, and interview preparation workshops.

5. How does Eduplier help me with NMIMS admissions?

Eduplier provides free academic guidance to help you select appropriate specializations, check admission eligibility, organize required documentation, and submit your official application without extra charges.
