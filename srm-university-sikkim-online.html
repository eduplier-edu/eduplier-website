Accreditations and Approvals

What to check before you enrol in any online program.

  • UGC recognition. The university lists UGC 2(f) approval and publishes its UGC disclosures on its official site.
  • NAAC accreditation. The university displays a NAAC certificate on its website. Check the grade and validity date there.
  • AICTE approval. Technical and management programs are subject to AICTE approval for the academic year.
  • UGC-DEB entitlement. For online degrees, confirm that the specific program is entitled for your session on the UGC-DEB website.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Eduplier the official website of SRM University Sikkim?

No. Eduplier is an independent education guidance platform. Admissions, fees, exams and degrees are managed by SRM University Sikkim. Visit srmus.ac.in for official information.

2. Are SRM University Sikkim online degrees valid?

Online degrees are valid when the university and the specific program are entitled by UGC-DEB for the relevant academic session. Verify this on the official UGC-DEB website before you apply.

3. Which online programs does SRM University Sikkim offer?

Programs commonly listed include online MBA, MCA, BBA and BCA. Availability and specializations change by session, so confirm the current list with the university or an Eduplier counsellor.

4. What is the fee for the online programs?

Fees differ by program, specialization and intake and can change. Ask for the latest official fee structure, including exam and other charges, before paying.

5. Can I study while working?

Yes. Online programs are designed for flexible learning, although assignments and examinations still follow the university schedule.
