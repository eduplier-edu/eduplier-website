Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding Shoolini University admissions.

What is the eligibility criteria for UG and PG programs?

UG candidates generally require 50–55%+ marks in 10+2 from a recognised board (PCM/PCB for science and engineering). PG courses require a recognised bachelor’s degree in the relevant discipline. Specific programs also evaluate scores from national entrance tests.

Does Shoolini University offer online degrees?

Yes. In addition to campus programs, Shoolini offers UGC-DEB approved online degrees including Online MBA, BBA, BCA, and MCA designed for working professionals and distance learners.

Are merit-based scholarships available?

Yes, scholarships are provided based on academic merit, CUET/JEE/CAT percentiles, single girl child quotas, sports achievements, and defence wards.
