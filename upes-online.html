Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about online degrees and admission assistance.

Are online degrees valid for government jobs & higher education?

Yes. Under official UGC regulations, online degrees obtained from UGC-DEB entitled universities hold full equivalence with conventional on-campus degrees for private MNC roles, government competitive exams (UPSC/SSC), and further studies abroad.

Are classes live or recorded?

Both. Universities conduct live interactive sessions on weekends and upload high-definition recordings to your Learning Management System (LMS) so you can catch up at your own pace.

Are fee payments available in monthly installments (EMI)?

Yes. Most partner universities provide semester-wise fee payments as well as no-cost monthly EMI options through authorized education financing partners.

Do online students get placement support?

Yes. Students gain access to virtual career fairs, specialized job boards, ATS resume-building workshops, and mock interview prep conducted by career services.
