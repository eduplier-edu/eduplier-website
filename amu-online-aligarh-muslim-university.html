Check Recognition Before You Apply

Recognition is checked university by university, programme by programme and session by session.

  • UGC recognition. AMU is a central university recognised by the UGC.
  • NAAC accreditation. The university’s website shows grade A+ (3.35/4). Check the validity on the official site.
  • UGC-DEB entitlement. For online degrees, confirm that your exact programme is entitled for your admission session.
  • Official admission. Apply only through AMU’s official admission portal, never through an unofficial arrangement.
Verify before you pay: search for AMU and your programme on deb.ugc.ac.in. Reports differ on the mode and status of AMU’s programmes for 2026–27, so also read the latest notice from the university’s Centre for Distance and Online Education.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Eduplier the official website of Aligarh Muslim University?

No. Eduplier is an independent education guidance platform. Admissions, fees, exams and degrees are managed by Aligarh Muslim University. Visit amu.ac.in for official information and apply only through the university's official admission portal.

2. Does Aligarh Muslim University offer online degrees?

AMU offers online programmes through its Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE). The list of programmes and sessions changes, so confirm the current list on the university's official CDOE pages.

3. Are AMU online degrees UGC-DEB approved?

Approval is granted for each programme and academic session. Reports from different sources differ on AMU's mode and status for 2026–27, so check the university and your exact programme on deb.ugc.ac.in and the latest CDOE notice before you apply.

4. What is AMU's NAAC grade?

The university's official website shows NAAC accreditation at grade A+ (3.35 out of 4). Some third-party sites list a different grade, so rely on the official site.

5. Which programmes can I study online at AMU?

Commonly listed programmes include online BA, B.Com, MA and M.Com, with subject options that vary by session. Confirm availability and eligibility on the official CDOE portal.

6. What are the fees for AMU online programmes?

Fees differ by programme and session and can change. Check the official CDOE fee notice and ask for a full breakdown before you pay.

7. Can I study at AMU online while working?

Online programmes are designed for flexible learning, which suits working professionals. Assignments and examinations still follow the university's schedule.

8. Why should I verify a university before applying?

UGC has warned that franchise-style arrangements for online degrees are not permitted. Always confirm which university admits you, conducts the programme and awards the degree.
