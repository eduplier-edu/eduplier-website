About Aligarh Muslim University

Aligarh Muslim University traces its roots to the MAO College founded in 1875. It is a central university accredited by NAAC at grade A+ and has 13 faculties, from arts and commerce to law and engineering, with 117 departments. Its Centre for Distance and Online Education runs the university’s online programmes.

  • Flagship online programmes: BA, B.Com, MA and M.Com
  • Large campus: about 1,155 acres, with 20 halls of residence
  • NAAC A+ accredited, per the university’s official website
  • Big alumni network of around 170,000, as published by the university

Source: amu.ac.in. Confirm figures on the official website.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about admissions, degree validity and recognition.

Is an online degree from Aligarh Muslim University valid?

Online degrees are valid when the university and the specific programme are entitled by UGC-DEB for the relevant academic session. AMU is a UGC-recognised central university with NAAC A+ accreditation, but reports on its online and distance status for 2026–27 differ. Please verify your exact programme on the official UGC-DEB portal and in the latest CDOE notice before applying.

Which online programmes does AMU offer?

Commonly listed programmes include Online BA, B.Com, MA and M.Com, with subject options that vary by session. Our counsellors can help you check which programmes are currently open for admission.

Are AMU online degrees valid for government jobs?

UGC-DEB entitled online degrees are treated on par with regular degrees under UGC regulations. Always check the specific recruiter's eligibility notice, and confirm that your programme was entitled for your admission session.

How do I apply for AMU online admission?

Apply through the official admission portal of AMU's Centre for Distance and Online Education and pay fees only through the university. Eduplier provides free guidance but does not conduct admissions.

What are the fees for AMU online programmes?

Fees differ by programme and session and can change. Check the official CDOE fee notice and ask for a full breakdown, including examination and other charges, before you pay.

Can I study at AMU online while working?

Online programmes are designed for flexible learning, which suits working professionals. Assignments and examinations still follow the university's schedule.

Why should I verify a university before applying?

UGC has warned that franchise-style arrangements for online degrees are not permitted. Always confirm which university admits you, conducts the programme and awards the degree.

How does Eduplier help me?

Eduplier offers free, transparent counselling. We assess your career goals, help you shortlist programmes, explain eligibility and show you how to verify recognition before you apply.
